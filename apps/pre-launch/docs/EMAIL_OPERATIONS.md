# Email delivery and launch campaigns

**Status** Proposed

## Message catalogue

| Type | Trigger and proposed subject | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verify | Successful first admission or bounded resend · “Confirm your Meduzellar early access” | Current token, expiry and expected subscription purpose. No open tracking or URL rewriting. |
| Confirmed | First successful verification per generation · “You're on the Meduzellar waitlist!” | Plain language and an unsubscribe link. Suppress if no longer eligible at dispatch. |
| Official launch | Approved live campaign only · “Meduzellar is officially live!” | Immutable reviewed content and approved Explore Meduzellar URL. Include visible unsubscribe, one-click headers and approved sender identification. |
| Privacy proof | Authorized deletion case · “Confirm your Meduzellar privacy request” | Dedicated short-lived owner proof. No enrollment or campaign side effects. Suppressed or unreachable addresses go through the verified support workflow. |
| Test | Named internal test recipient and explicit operator action | Clearly labeled TEST, isolated test logical keys and production checks. Test sends use inert links or test credentials and never touch live membership. |

Render branded HTML and plain text from versioned immutable templates. Avoid subscriber personalization beyond the delivery address. Authenticate the sender, validate DKIM coverage on one-click headers in delivered MIME, and review SPF, DMARC, replies and deliverability before live send. Keep consent and suppression in the application; honor provider suppression too. ## Durable worker

1. Enqueue a domain change and job in the same DB transaction. Due job admission is database-owned with a uniqueness constraint. Scheduler calls a no-store authenticated route on a one-minute proposed cadence. Missed triggers are caught by future ticks because due work remains queryable.
2. Claim due rows using a short transaction with parameterized `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, random lease tokens, time-bounded leases and capped work. Recover expired leases. Reserve capacity for transactional work, and exclude a second overlapping worker with a database lease plus per-job fencing. Stop claiming within a conservative function deadline. Await provider calls and persist results before returning.
3. Before sending, use a short transaction to re-read recipient, generation, consent, suppression, campaign state, deadlines and dispatch budget. Write an attempt and dispatch authorization marker. A pause, unsubscribe, deletion or cancellation committed before the marker blocks handoff. A concurrent change after handoff authorization can still race an in-flight provider call. Bound the window, surface in-flight counts and disclose this operational limit. Never hold a database transaction open across the provider network call.
4. Submit one message per stable provider idempotency key derived from the delivery identity. Reuse the same canonical payload, content version, links and key. Freeze the versioned payload components before the first attempt and retain them only as long as necessary. Include a non-PII correlation tag when the provider supports it. Commit results only with the active lease token. An accepted provider response is `ACCEPTED`, not inbox delivery.
5. Retry with bounded jittered exponential delay for confirmed retryable errors and provider throttle. Respect `Retry-After`, global team quota and the applicable token/campaign deadline. Proposed schedule is 1, 5, 15, 45 and 120 min, maximum six attempts. Treat timeout, disconnect and uncertain HTTP results as possible acceptance. Never change key or payload. Anchor the safe retry horizon to the earliest possible provider acceptance, with proposed hard cutoff at 23 hours for Resend's 24-hour key lifetime.
6. Hold uncertain work after that horizon as `UNKNOWN`. Reconcile via provider ID, supported correlation metadata, signed events and operator investigation. Never blindly resubmit an ambiguous live send after provider deduplication expires or switch providers for that send. Confirmed permanent failures stop and alert if systemic. Force retry after reliable evidence of non-acceptance needs explicit recovery policy and audited review.

The delivery status progression is QUEUED → LEASED → ACCEPTED → DELIVERED, with RETRY, FAILED, SKIPPED, CANCELED and UNKNOWN for alternative paths. DELAYED, BOUNCED and COMPLAINED are provider events and remain visible even after an earlier delivery event. Accepted or potentially accepted mail cannot reliably be recalled. ## Official launch workflow

- Create one campaign with revisions in DRAFT. Freeze template, localized copy, approved sender, destination and digest at preview. Output estimate and time. An authorized operator sends a bounded internal test to an allowlisted destination. Persist test acceptance/delivery, actor and revision.
- Owner performs fresh second-factor verification and confirms the exact revision and maximum recipient count after reviewing a successful test. Verification takes a shared lock on the singleton enrollment epoch before the subscriber lock and stamps the epoch on activation. Approval takes an exclusive lock on the epoch before counting, records the cutoff epoch, advances it and atomically records the approval, audit and materialization job. Require the actual count within the cap. Bind mutation idempotency to the same content digest. This separates concurrent verification commits from the approved audience and keeps fan-out out of the request.
- Materialize in keyset chunks from stable subscriber IDs. Include only the current active generation whose verified epoch is at or below the approval cutoff. Persist cursor, cap and unique (campaign, subscriber) identity. Late activations remain outside this campaign. Dispatch can shrink after unsubscribe or suppression. Persist progress counts and report final scope.
- Campaign transitions DRAFT → APPROVED → MATERIALIZING → RUNNING → COMPLETED, with PAUSED and CANCELED. Pause preserves queued work and prevents additional authorizations. Resume reuses the approved revision and cap. Cancel permanently excludes unsent work; jobs and in-flight outcomes remain auditable. Deployment, cron and webhook processing independently lack authority to approve a campaign.
- Operator status shows queued, accepted, delivered, retry, skipped/suppressed, canceled, permanent failures and unknown. Alert on backlog/oldest age, no worker heartbeat, no verification progress, quota and spike in hard bounces or complaints. Pause campaigns while unknown or severe deliverability signals are reviewed. Log IDs and coarse failure classes only.

## Provider callbacks and recovery

Verify signature over bounded raw bytes, configured timestamp tolerance and authenticated event ID. Persist and deduplicate before acknowledging. Project only required fields, process unknown IDs later, and maintain an event precedence table so out-of-order events never clear suppression or regress delivered state back to accepted. Hard bounce and complaint add suppression transactionally and prevent future jobs; soft delays are monitored and the provider owns post-acceptance delivery retries. Validate mappings for queued, delayed, delivered, bounced, complained, suppressed and failed events in a sandbox before release. Track provider-ID reconciliation and alert on missing event coverage. [Resend webhook verification](https://resend.com/changelog/managing-webhooks-via-api)

Reconcile after interruptions with scheduler health, recent event IDs, queued leases, timestamps and state counts. Restore procedures keep dispatch disabled until deletions, opt-outs and ambiguous deliveries are reconciled.
